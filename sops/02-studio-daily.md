# SOP-02 — Studio daily (café owner)

**Audience:** café owner / manager  
**When:** prices change, a dish is added, something is sold out, hours change.

## Open Studio

1. On the café phone or laptop, open the Studio link you were given (`…/studio`).
2. Enter the password.
3. You land on **Dishes**.

If you stay logged in, you usually will not need the password again for about 12 hours on that same browser.

## Change a price

1. Tap the dish.
2. Change the ₹ amount.
3. Tap **Publish** at the top.

## Add a dish

1. On **Dishes**, tap **Add dish**.
2. Type the name (required).
3. Pick the category.
4. Type the price in rupees (numbers only).
5. Short description if you have one.
6. Set diet: Veg / Non-veg / Vegan / Egg.
7. Optional: **Choose** a photo from the phone (JPG/PNG/WebP, **max 4 MB**). It is shrunk for the menu.
8. Leave **Available** on unless it is sold out.
9. Tap **Publish**.

If Publish is blocked, a dish is missing a name — open it and name it.

## Hide a sold-out dish (keep it for later)

1. Open the dish.
2. Turn **Available** off.
3. **Publish**.

Guests still see it, faded, with **Not available**. To take it off the menu completely, delete the dish, then Publish.

## Delete a dish

1. Open the dish.
2. Delete it.
3. **Publish**.

## Categories

1. Open the **Categories** tab.
2. Add, rename, or reorder sections.
3. Dishes stay in the category you assigned.
4. **Publish**.

Empty categories still show as tabs. Hide unused ones by deleting the category after moving or deleting its dishes.

## Café details (name, WhatsApp, hours)

1. Open the **Café** tab.
2. Edit name, tagline, WhatsApp (digits with country code, e.g. `91XXXXXXXXXX`), address, maps, hours, Instagram.
3. **Publish**.

WhatsApp is required. There is no phone-call button on the guest menu.

## Before you leave Studio

Look at the top:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Unsaved changes | Tables still have the old menu. Tap **Publish**. |
| Publishing… | Wait. |
| Live for all tables | Guests will see this. |
| Couldn’t publish | Try again, or name every dish / fill café name + WhatsApp. |

Then open the **guest menu** (not Studio) on a phone and spot-check one price.

## Do not

- Do not edit on a guest’s phone and walk away without locking (lock icon in the header).
- Do not tap **Reset to The Cafe Store** or **Load Hashtag Cafe demo** unless you mean to replace the whole draft — and even then you must Publish for it to hit tables.
