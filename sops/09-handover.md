# SOP-09 — Handover checklist

**Audience:** you (agency)  
**When:** the café is ready to put QRs on tables.

## Before the meeting

- [ ] New repo + new Netlify site (not the master demo)
- [ ] Guest URL works on your phone
- [ ] Studio URL works
- [ ] Sample **The Cafe Store** name / placeholder WhatsApp / placeholder address is gone
- [ ] Real name, WhatsApp, address, hours, maps
- [ ] Categories match the kitchen
- [ ] Prices in ₹ match the printed/laminated list if they have one
- [ ] Veg / non-veg marks correct
- [ ] Sold-out items marked unavailable (not missing by accident)
- [ ] Theme + logo (or name-only) signed off
- [ ] CSV export saved as a backup on your side
- [ ] At least one test QR scanned in café lighting

## With the owner (15 minutes)

1. Open **guest menu** — this is what customers see.
2. Open **Studio** — they set or confirm the password (SOP-06).
3. Together: change one price → **Publish** → refresh a second phone.
4. Together: mark one dish unavailable → Publish → confirm “Not available”.
5. Show lock icon.
6. Show Café tab (hours, WhatsApp) and warn that **Reset to The Cafe Store** / **Load Hashtag Cafe demo** replaces the draft.
7. Send files:

   **Message 1 — links**
   - Guest menu URL (for QRs and Instagram bio)
   - Studio URL
   - Owner quick card (`sops/cafe-owner-quick-card.md`)

   **Message 2 — password only** (separate chat / in person)

8. Agree who is allowed to Publish (usually owner + one manager).

## After

- [ ] Print QRs (SOP-07)
- [ ] You do not keep the password unless they pay you to operate Studio
- [ ] Bookmark their Netlify + GitHub, not the master template
- [ ] Optional: custom domain, then reprint QRs

## Done when

A stranger’s phone can scan a table code, read a dish, see the right ₹, and WhatsApp the café — without ever seeing Studio.
